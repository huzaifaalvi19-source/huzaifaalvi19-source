## About me

I am a PEC-registered Industrial Engineer and a BSc graduate of **University of Engineering and Technology, Taxila, Pakistan**. I enjoy turning practical engineering questions into designs, experiments, simulation models, and useful analysis.

My work spans a recyclable-filament extrusion prototype, research comparing conventional and EDM-machined tooling, manufacturing-system simulation, facility planning, and business analytics. I am interested in connecting this foundation with intelligent manufacturing, robotics, and applied AI.
